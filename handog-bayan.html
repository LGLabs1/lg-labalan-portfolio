Handog Bayan

Handog Bayan is a request-donation platform designed to make it easier for communities to request and deliver help where it's needed most.

Overview

Handog Bayan is a community-based request and donation platform designed to connect individuals in need with donors who are willing to help. The system streamlines the process of submitting requests, making donations, and monitoring transactions through a centralized digital platform.

The platform allows users to create and manage assistance requests, while donors can easily browse verified requests and contribute through the system. To ensure transparency and security, administrators are provided with tools to validate requests, monitor user activity, and generate reports for analysis.

Handog Bayan aims to improve community support by making the donation process more accessible, organized, and accountable, reducing manual coordination and enhancing trust between donors, recipients, and administrators.

Core features

  • User Management
    • Registration, login, and logout for new and existing users.
    • Profile management with view/edit profile details.
    • Update contact information and basic account settings.
  • Requests & Donations
    • Create requests or posts asking for help, resources, or donations.
    • Browse a list of recent and open requests with search and filters.
    • View full request details and edit, update, or cancel your own requests.
    • Create donations to offer help or resources to specific requests.
    • Track donations across pending, active, and completed states.
    • See a history or log of donations given and support received.
  • Messaging & Communication
    • Send one-on-one messages related to specific requests.
    • View message threads to keep conversations organized.
    • Receive notifications for request updates, new messages, and donation status changes.
  • Voting & Feedback
    • Upvote or downvote requests to signal urgency or popularity.
    • View vote counts for each request at a glance.
    • Leave feedback or comments on individual requests.
  • Feed & Home dashboard
    • See the most recent or most urgent requests in a multi-row layout.
    • Highlight requests based on votes, urgency, or recent activity.
    • Use quick actions to donate, message the requester, or share a request.
  • Admin & moderation tools
    • Manage users by verifying accounts or applying bans and suspensions.
    • Review and approve or reject new requests before they appear in the feed.
    • Use a moderation dashboard to review flagged content or suspicious activity.
  • File & media management
    • Attach images or documents to requests and donations.
    • Preview and view attachments directly from the request detail.
  • Analytics & reporting
    • Show personal stats such as donations made, donations received, and fulfilled requests.
    • Provide community impact reports on the admin side.
